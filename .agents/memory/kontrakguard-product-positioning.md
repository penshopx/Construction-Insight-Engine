---
name: KontrakGuard product positioning
description: Durable product direction and sequencing for the construction-contract platform.
---

Treat KontrakGuard as a construction-contract legal-defense and decision-support system, not as a generic reference library or document repository. Lead with legal deadlines, obligations, evidence continuity, VO/EOT/claim/NCR workflows, and exposure visibility.

**Why:** The approved blueprints sharpened the product around preventing loss of contractual rights and improving dispute readiness. The user explicitly chose to restructure the application around this direction.

**How to apply:** Keep SKKNI and legal references as supporting modules. Prioritize reliable workflow and evidence foundations before advanced AI, predictive scoring, blockchain anchoring, or claims of court admissibility. Always qualify legal and forensic outputs.
