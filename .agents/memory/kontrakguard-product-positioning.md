---
name: KontrakGuard product positioning
description: Durable product direction and sequencing for the construction-contract platform.
---

Treat KontrakGuard as a collaborative construction-dispute platform with three connected pillars: contract law and administration; building/construction failure; and Penilai Ahli services for consultation, assistance/advocacy, and mediation. It is not a generic reference library or document repository.

**Why:** The approved product direction expands legal-defense workflows into constructive resolution: detect issues early, preserve evidence, combine technical and contractual assessment, and involve independent experts before disputes escalate.

**How to apply:** Keep SKKNI and legal references as supporting foundations. Distinguish independent expert assessment from party advocacy, and never imply that every Penilai Ahli is a licensed advocate or certified mediator. Prioritize reliable workflow and evidence foundations before advanced AI, predictive scoring, institutional integrations, or claims of court admissibility. Always qualify legal, forensic, qualification, and verification outputs.
