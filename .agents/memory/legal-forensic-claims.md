---
name: Legal and forensic claims
description: Guardrails for product copy about legal status, evidence integrity, regulations, and risk scoring.
---

Treat legal references as searchable source material, not a determination of current validity or applicability, unless an authoritative official-source validation process exists. Describe evidence records as administrative metadata unless the system actually preserves original files and implements verifiable integrity and custody controls. Keep risk scores clearly labeled as illustrative until their model and methodology are validated.

**Why:** Product language can cause users to rely on the application for legal conclusions, forensic integrity, or quantified risk that the current implementation does not establish.

**How to apply:** Review new UI copy and workflows for claims about legal advice, compliance, admissibility, chain of custody, immutability, expert findings, and risk predictions. Use explicit limitations until each capability has supporting implementation and validation.
